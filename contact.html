FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start investing in global markets?

+

Simply contact our team for a free consultation. We'll help you open an international brokerage account, complete KYC, and guide you through your first investment. The entire process can be completed within 3-5 business days.

What is the minimum investment for unlisted shares?

+

The minimum investment varies by company. Some unlisted shares are available for as low as one lot (typically 25-100 shares). Contact us for current pricing and lot sizes for specific companies.

Is my investment safe with WealthBridge?

+

Absolutely. We work with SEBI-registered partners and internationally regulated institutions. All share transfers are done through official NSDL/CDSL channels, and your funds are held in segregated accounts with top-tier banks.

How does the Golden Visa process work?

+

We provide end-to-end support. Starting with a free consultation to understand your goals, we then guide you through selecting the right program (UAE or USA EB-5), making the qualifying investment, documentation, and visa application. Most programs are processed within 30-90 days.
